1.Introduction and scope

Krozenda ("Krozenda", "we", "us", "our") operates an online marketplace on which retail customers and business buyers purchase products from independent sellers, dropshipping partners and from Krozenda's own inventory. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect from you, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

This policy applies to:

  • the Krozenda website at [https://www.krozenda.com];
  • the Krozenda Buyer mobile applications for Android and iOS;
  • the Krozenda Seller mobile applications for Android and iOS;
  • the Seller Panel and Admin Panel used by our vendors and staff; and
  • any email, SMS, push notification or customer-support interaction that arises out of your use of the above.

It applies to everyone who uses the platform: retail (B2C) customers, business (B2B) buyers such as dealers, distributors, traders and wholesalers, marketplace sellers, dropshipping partners, manufacturers, companies, and visitors who browse without registering.

By creating an account, placing an order, listing products, or otherwise using the platform, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. Where the law requires your consent, we will ask for it separately and clearly — your continued use of the platform is not by itself treated as consent to anything for which the law requires a distinct, informed choice.

4.Personal data we collect

We collect only the data we need to run the marketplace. What we collect depends on who you are and what you do.

4.1 Account and identity data (all users)

  • Full name.
  • Mobile number, used for OTP-based login and order notifications.
  • Email address, used for verification, order confirmations and account notices.
  • Password — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash. We never store, and cannot recover, your plaintext password.
  • Your role and capabilities on the platform (for example: retail customer, B2B buyer, seller, dropshipping partner) and your account status.
  • Profile photo and display preferences, if you choose to add them.

4.2 Order, delivery and transaction data (buyers)

  • Delivery addresses saved in your address book — recipient name, contact number, full address, landmark, city, state and PIN code.
  • Billing address and, for B2B buyers, GSTIN and registered business name for tax invoicing.
  • Order history: products ordered, quantity, price paid, applicable taxes and discounts, order status, and the seller each item was fulfilled by.
  • Shipment and tracking details, including AWB number, courier assigned and delivery status.
  • Returns, replacement and cancellation requests, and the reasons you give for them.
  • Refund records and payment references.

4.3 Seller and business data (sellers and dropshipping partners)

  • Store name, business model, business type and contact details of the authorised person.
  • Pickup and warehouse addresses.
  • KYC documents — see section 10 for the full list and how they are handled.
  • Bank account details, or the payment-partner linked-account identifier used to settle your sales proceeds.
  • Commission, settlement and payout records.
  • Product listings, inventory and pricing data you upload.

4.4 Content you create

  • Product reviews, star ratings and any photographs you attach to a review.
  • Questions, support tickets, complaints and the correspondence relating to them.
  • Wishlist, cart contents and saved items.

4.5 Technical and usage data

  • IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device model and device identifiers.
  • Application and server log data — pages and screens viewed, features used, referring URL, date and time of access, and error diagnostics.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push token, used only to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used for delivery serviceability checks and fraud prevention. We do not collect precise GPS location unless you explicitly grant that permission in the mobile app for address auto-fill, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

4.6 Consent and compliance records

When you accept our Terms & Conditions, this Privacy Policy, the Vendor Agreement, or our Shipping and Return policies, we record the fact of acceptance together with the timestamp, the IP address from which it was given, and the version of the document you accepted. We keep this record as proof of consent, which the law requires us to be able to demonstrate.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your card number, CVV, card expiry, UPI PIN, net-banking password or any other payment credential — these are entered directly on our payment partner's secure interface and never reach Krozenda's servers. We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not use your data to build profiles for third-party advertisers.

7.Consent and how to withdraw it

Where we rely on your consent, we ask for it through a clear affirmative action — for example, ticking a checkbox at registration or opting in to marketing messages. We do not use pre-ticked boxes.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, and withdrawing it is as easy as giving it. You can do so from your account settings, by using the unsubscribe or STOP option in any marketing message, or by writing to our Grievance Officer at the address in section 22.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of anything we did before you withdrew it. Please also note that some data is necessary for the platform to function, or is required by law: if you withdraw consent for that data, we may no longer be able to provide you with the relevant service, and we may still need to retain certain records — tax invoices, for example — for the periods described in section 14.

9.Payment information

All payments on Krozenda are made online through our payment gateway. Supported methods include UPI, credit card, debit card, net banking and wallets.

  • Card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs and net-banking credentials are entered on the payment gateway's own PCI-DSS compliant interface. Krozenda never receives, sees or stores them.
  • We store only the payment reference identifier, the amount, the payment status and the timestamp, so that we can reconcile your order, process refunds and maintain accounting records.
  • Where the gateway offers tokenisation for saved cards, the token is held by the gateway under RBI's card-on-file tokenisation rules, not by us.
  • Vendor proceeds are settled through the gateway's split-settlement facility directly to the seller's verified linked account. To enable this, a seller's business and bank details are shared with the gateway for its own KYC.

10.KYC and business verification data

Sellers, dropshipping partners, manufacturers and companies must complete verification before their storefront is activated. Depending on the category and business type, we collect:

  • PAN card;
  • Aadhaar or another accepted government photo identity document;
  • GST registration certificate;
  • FSSAI licence, for sellers listing food products;
  • a cancelled cheque or bank account proof;
  • address proof for the registered and pickup addresses; and
  • business incorporation or partnership documents, where applicable.

How this data is handled:

  • KYC documents are reviewed manually by our authorised Admin staff. Access is restricted to the staff members whose role requires it, and that access is logged.
  • Documents are stored in encrypted form and are never published, listed, or made visible to buyers or to other sellers.
  • Where an identity document is used, we collect it only for the stated verification purpose, retain only what is necessary, and handle it in accordance with the law governing that document. We do not use it as a general-purpose identifier across the platform.
  • We may be required to retain KYC and related transaction records for the statutory periods described in section 14, even after a seller account is closed.

11.Sellers as independent data handlers

Krozenda is a marketplace. For products sold by independent sellers and dropshipping partners, the contract of sale is between you and that seller. Once we pass on the delivery details necessary to fulfil your order, the seller also becomes responsible, in its own right, for how it handles that data — including its own invoicing, packaging and courier handover.

We require every seller, as a condition of onboarding, to handle buyer data lawfully, to use it only for order fulfilment, to keep it secure, and not to retain it longer than necessary. We are not, however, responsible for a seller's own independent privacy practices outside the platform. If you believe a seller has misused your data, tell us through the grievance channel in section 22 and we will investigate and act, which may include suspending or removing the seller.

13.Marketing, SMS, email and push notifications

We distinguish clearly between two kinds of messages:

  • Transactional messages — OTPs, order confirmations, dispatch and delivery updates, refund and settlement notices, security alerts, and policy changes. These are part of the service. You cannot opt out of them while you hold an active account, because they are how we tell you what is happening with your money and your orders.
  • Promotional messages — offers, discounts, recommendations and newsletters. These are sent only if you opt in, and every one carries an unsubscribe link, a STOP keyword, or a notification toggle you can switch off at any time.

All transactional SMS is sent through TRAI DLT-registered sender IDs using pre-approved templates, as Indian telecom regulation requires. You can disable push notifications entirely from your device's operating-system settings.

15.How we protect your data

We apply technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption in transit — all traffic between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest — KYC documents and other sensitive data are stored encrypted.
  • Password protection — passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, and are never recoverable in readable form.
  • Access control — role-based permissions restrict staff access to the minimum needed for their job, with separate Super Admin, Admin and Staff privilege levels.
  • Audit trails — administrative actions on sensitive records are logged.
  • Platform hardening — security headers, rate limiting on authentication and OTP endpoints, input validation, and protection against common web attacks.
  • Payment isolation — payment credentials are handled entirely by our PCI-DSS compliant gateway and never touch our infrastructure.
  • Backups — regular encrypted backups, with tested restoration.
  • Vendor diligence — service providers are contractually bound to confidentiality and security obligations.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. You also have a part to play: keep your password confidential, never share your OTP with anyone (Krozenda staff will never ask you for it), and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

17.Children's data

Krozenda is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and transact on the platform. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Where processing a child's data becomes unavoidable, we will obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and we will not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it promptly.

18.Cross-border transfers

Our primary data storage and processing takes place in India. Some of our service providers may process limited data on servers outside India — push-notification delivery infrastructure, for example. Where that happens:

  • we transfer only the minimum data needed for that service;
  • we do not transfer personal data to any country or territory that the Central Government has restricted for this purpose; and
  • we require the recipient by contract to apply protections equivalent to those in this policy.

19.Data breach handling

We maintain an incident-response process for suspected personal data breaches. In the event of a breach we will contain and investigate it, assess the risk to affected individuals, and notify the Data Protection Board of India and every affected Data Principal in the form and within the timelines prescribed by law. We will also report the incident to CERT-In where the Information Technology Act and its directions require it. Our notice will describe, so far as we know it, the nature and extent of the breach, its likely consequences, the measures we have taken, and what you can do to protect yourself.
