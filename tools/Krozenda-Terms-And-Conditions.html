Client action required — consumer law reconciliation. Before publication, your legal counsel should confirm: (a) that the No-Return default with these three exceptions is defensible for every category you intend to list; (b) whether a short cooling-off window is required or commercially advisable; and (c) the correct disclosure wording on each product page. 11.Return to Origin (RTO)

A shipment may be returned to the Seller undelivered — an incorrect or incomplete address, the recipient unreachable after the courier's attempts, or refusal to accept the delivery. The No-Return policy does not apply to RTO; the following does:

  • Where the RTO is not attributable to you — courier failure or a Seller error — you are refunded in full, including shipping.
  • Where the RTO is attributable to you — a wrong address, persistent unavailability, or refusal without a reason under section 10.1 — you are refunded the product value, and we may deduct the forward and return shipping actually incurred, which is disclosed to you before the deduction is made.
  • Repeated buyer-attributable RTOs may lead us to restrict or suspend the account under section 19.
  • On completion of an RTO, the Seller's settlement for that Sub-Order is reversed and inventory is restored.
